What Edu is reading this week (Dec 8 - 14, 2025)

Here is the roundup of links for this week:

Cloud & Infrastructure

  • IBM to acquire Confluent - Major industry news as IBM announces its acquisition of the data streaming platform Confluent.

General & Misc

  • Turtletoy - A platform for generative art designed to be drawn by plotters.
  • Super Mario 64 for PS1 - A GitHub project porting the classic Nintendo 64 title to the original PlayStation.
